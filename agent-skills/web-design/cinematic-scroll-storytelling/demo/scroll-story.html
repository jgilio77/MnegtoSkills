01 — Method

Three rules keep the page readable while it moves.

One focal point

Each section leads with a single idea. Everything else steps back.

Motion after meaning

Animation starts once the text is understood, never before.

Play once

Reveals animate a single time so the page stays calm on the second pass.

Scroll sets the pace.

Nothing moves on its own.

Each line earns its place.
